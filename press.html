Long · 140 words

Zenna Lua (b. 1991, Greve Strand) is a photographic artist and psychotherapist based in Copenhagen. She studied at Engelsholm Kunsthøjskole and trained as a psychotherapist at Dansk NLP Center, and has run both practices independently since 2018.

Her photographs are made in natural light, handheld or on a tripod, and are not staged. Each series follows a single subject until it yields: Like a flower you shall bloom photographs macro botanicals a day past their best hour; Gigantically Subtle records leaf shadows crossing one plastered wall across a single August afternoon; Greve, at the hour of gold returns to the same stretch of Danish coast for the last twenty minutes of light.

Alongside the photographic work she holds guided evenings and seasonal circles in Copenhagen, where the images are printed and handled rather than hung.
