Series

Three bodies of work

Each one is a single subject followed until it gave something up: a flower at full opening, a wall through one afternoon, a horizon in the last twenty minutes of light.

Prefer everything in one grid? All works →

  1. Series 01

    Like a flower you shall bloom

    Macro botanicals photographed a day past their best hour — poppy, clematis, hollyhock, and the seed heads that come after. Eighteen frames, shown 23 April – 31 May.

    18 frames · Exhibition · 60 × 75 and 90 × 120 cm

  2. Series 02

    Gigantically Subtle

    One plastered wall, one fig in a pot, one August afternoon, photographed at twenty-minute intervals until the sun dropped behind the roof opposite. Hung as a single line, 14:30 to 17:40.

    9 frames · Ongoing · 50 × 62 cm each

  3. Series 03

    Greve, at the hour of gold

    The last light over Køge Bugt, shot from the beach I grew up on. Most evenings the sea is grey and I go home; roughly one in six gives something.

    6 shown · Ongoing · 90 × 60 cm

Mirror work

The self-portraits sit outside the three series — they belong to the gatherings rather than to a body of work. They are under Mirror in the gallery, and the evening they come from is I See You.
